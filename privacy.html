Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

OMNICAST ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted application that runs entirely on the creator's own computer. It is used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with your permission (OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly), the app uploads videos you choose to your YouTube channel, sets their title, description, tags, privacy status and thumbnail, and reads your channel name to show which account is connected.
  • TikTok and Instagram: if you connect them, the app uses their official APIs only to publish the videos you choose and to read your account name.
  • Your videos: files you upload are processed locally on your computer (frame analysis and thumbnail generation).

How data is stored

Access tokens and video files are stored only on your own computer, in the app's local data folder. Nothing is sent to any server operated by OMNICAST — there is none. Data is sent only directly to the platform you publish to (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), at your request.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising. The app does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train AI/ML models.

Google API Services

OMNICAST's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads to YouTube are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access & deleting data

You can disconnect any platform inside the app at any time, and revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens and files.
