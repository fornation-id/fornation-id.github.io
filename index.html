OMNICAST

One upload → YouTube, TikTok & Instagram Reels.

OMNICAST is a personal, self-hosted tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It helps a creator publish their own videos to their own channels:

  • Analyzes the uploaded video locally to pick the best frames.
  • Suggests titles, descriptions, tags and captions for each platform.
  • Generates thumbnails from the video's own frames.
  • Uploads the video to the creator's own YouTube channel (and other platforms) only when the creator presses Launch.

OMNICAST is not a hosted service and does not collect or share user data. Read the Privacy Policy.
